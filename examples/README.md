# Examples

- [`gate_a_3d/`](gate_a_3d/) is the standalone Godot 4.6.1 acceptance scene
  for deterministic, explicitly routed 3D impact Foley. Wood, metal, and stone
  source materials strike a stone target through three ordered pair routes. It
  uses project-authored synthetic PCM fixtures and no downloaded audio or model.

Examples must remain minimal, redistributable, independently licensed, and
explicit about their engine version and evidence boundary.
